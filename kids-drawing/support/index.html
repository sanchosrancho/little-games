Support

Need help? Email littlegamesios@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version and a short description. Please do not include children's personal information.

What is free?

Blank paper for unrestricted creativity, one picture in each of the 12 themes, numbers 1–3 and letters A–C. All tools, spoken hints and the gallery are included. One optional purchase unlocks the other 90 lessons.

Small details and brush size

Use the plus button to enlarge the picture. Select Brush or Erase and choose one of four sizes. The brush preview shows its diameter. On Blank paper, the brush draws freely; on a picture, it stays inside the shape where you start.

Restore your purchase

Open For Parents on the home screen, solve the multiplication question and find Full drawing library below Voice & music. Tap Restore purchases while connected to the internet and using the same Apple Account.

No sound?

Check your device's silent mode and volume. In For Parents, check Spoken guidance, Lo-fi music and Music volume. Voice and music have separate switches.

Saving drawings

Tap the finish checkmark and Save. Artwork is kept in My Drawings on this device. Removing the app removes its local gallery. A purchase refund does not delete artwork.
