Support

Little Lumi. A little help, whenever you need it.

Updated: 23 October 2026

Get in touch

Email littlegamesios@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Lumi version, and a short description. A screenshot or short recording can help. Please do not include passwords or payment details.

How do I play?

Lumi jumps automatically. Slide your finger left or right, or choose Tilt in Settings. Double tap for one extra jump before landing. Springs launch you higher; a water rocket gives three seconds of fast flight. Catch a rare rainbow above the water to attract coins.

How do coves work?

Land on a platform with a flag to unlock a cove. Choose an unlocked cove in Worlds & coves to start a new run there. After a run, the return option costs 20 earned coins; starting a new run is free.

How do I change language?

Lumi supports English and Russian, with English as the default. It follows the app language on your iPhone. In iOS Settings, open Apps → Lumi → Language, if available, and choose your preferred language. Close and reopen the game after changing it.

Pearls, upgrades, and purchases

My Lumi → Pearls shows Apple’s current prices before confirmation. Pearl packs add 80, 220, or 600 pearls. Pearl Starter gives 100 bonus pearls once, Pearl Lumi, and a home fountain. Powers can also be upgraded with earned coins. There is no subscription.

Restore purchases

Use My Lumi → Pearls → Restore purchases with the same Apple Account and an internet connection. The starter’s permanent items can be restored; the same saved purchase does not refill spent bonus pearls. Spent consumable pearl packs do not restore through the App Store. The local wallet does not transfer between devices.

TestFlight and refunds

TestFlight purchases use Apple’s Sandbox and do not charge real money. After a Sandbox purchase, My Lumi → Pearls → Purchase testing offers Request a refund. A request must be approved by Apple before the balance changes. For ordinary purchases, see Apple’s refund instructions. We cannot approve Apple refunds.

Sound and performance

Music and effects have separate switches and volume sliders in Settings. The game respects Silent Mode. For smooth play, install the latest available build and try turning off Low Power Mode. If a slowdown repeats, send the build number and the moment it happens, such as during a rocket or rainbow.

Progress and reinstalling

Keep the game installed while we investigate a missing balance or progress issue. Ordinary progress is local and may be lost when the app is deleted; some Keychain purchase records may remain after reinstallation. There is no cross-device cloud save.
