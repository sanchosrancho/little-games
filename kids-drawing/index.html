A friendly voice along the way

Spoken guidance in English, Spanish, German, Russian, French and Brazilian Portuguese. Gentle lo-fi music can play in the background. Parents can choose the voice language, adjust the music or switch either off.

The app’s interface is in English. Requires iOS 17 or later.
