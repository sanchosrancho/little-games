Privacy policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026.

Kids Drawing is provided by Aleksandr Shevliakov. We do not collect personal data through the app.

Drawings and settings

Drawings, voice language and preferences are stored on your device. We do not upload them to a server. Device backups, if enabled, are controlled by your Apple settings. Deleting the app removes its local drawings and settings.

Purchases

Apple handles the optional in-app purchase and restoration through StoreKit. The app uses Apple's verified purchase status to unlock lessons. We do not receive your card details or other payment information. Apple processes transactions according to its own privacy policy. Restoration requires the Apple Account associated with the purchase.

Children and third-party services

The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, social features, web views or third-party SDKs. Speech and music are bundled locally. The app does not connect to ElevenLabs or music websites. Purchase controls and settings are behind a parental gate.

This website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no analytics or advertising scripts. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, to serve and secure the website.

Contact

If you email support, we use the information you choose to send to answer your request. Please do not send children's personal information. Contact littlegamesios@gmail.com with questions or requests about your support correspondence.
