SUPPORT · Kids Drawing for iPhone and iPad

A real person on the other end.

Email littlegamesios@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, app version and a short description of what happened. You can attach a screenshot with personal information removed. Please do not send children’s personal information.

Drawing, tracing and coloring

Choose Draw & Color, Numbers or Letters, then open a lesson. Follow the golden dot along the dotted line at your own pace. You can trace in either direction and lift your finger before continuing.

After tracing, use the drop to fill a shape or the brush to color inside its outline. Start a brush stroke inside the shape you want to paint. Use Undo to try again.

A free blank page

Open Blank paper on the home screen to draw freely without outlines. Choose a color and brush size, use Erase to remove part of a stroke, or Undo to reverse the last action. When you finish, save the drawing or start a fresh page.

Brush sizes and small details

There are 14 colors, including pink and black, and four brush sizes. The brush preview shows the selected diameter. The plus and minus magnifying-glass buttons enlarge or reduce the canvas; use a smaller brush for tiny details.

On guided pictures, brush strokes stay in the region where you start. On Blank paper, you can paint freely across the canvas.

What is free?

Blank paper is always free. The guided library includes 18 repeatable free lessons: one picture in each of 12 themes, numbers 1–3 and letters A–C. All drawing tools, spoken guidance and My Drawings are included.

One optional purchase unlocks the remaining 90 lessons, giving access to all 108 guided lessons. Blank paper is separate from that library. There is no subscription.

Unlocking the full library

Tap a locked lesson or open For Parents on the home screen. Solve the multiplication question to enter the parents’ area, then find Full drawing library below Voice & music. The purchase price is shown in your local currency before buying.

Apple handles payment. An internet connection is needed. If a purchase is awaiting approval, access opens after Apple confirms approval.

Restoring a purchase

In For Parents, open Full drawing library and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase and make sure the device is online. Restoring an eligible existing purchase does not charge you again.

If the store is unavailable, check your connection and App Store account, then try again. Include any displayed error message when contacting support; never send payment card details or passwords.

Saving your drawings

Tap the finish checkmark, then Save to My Drawings. Your artwork stays in My Drawings on this device. There is no in-app cloud sync or public gallery. Removing the app removes its local artwork; Apple-managed device backups are separate.

A purchase refund does not delete saved artwork, but it can remove access to paid lessons.

Voice languages and music

In For Parents → Voice & music, choose English, Spanish, German, Russian, French or Brazilian Portuguese. The interface remains in English. Spoken guidance and Lo-fi music have separate switches; Music volume adjusts the background.

If nothing plays, check device volume and silent mode as well as the app’s sound switches. Audio is bundled locally and does not need an internet connection.

Refunds

Apple handles payments and refund requests. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com and follow Apple’s instructions. We cannot issue an App Store refund directly.

Devices and offline use

Kids Drawing supports iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. Drawing, coloring, saving and bundled audio work offline. Buying or restoring the optional library unlock requires internet.
