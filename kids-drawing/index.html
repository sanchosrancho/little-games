Little lines. Big imagination.

Kids Drawing is a calm drawing and coloring app for iPhone and iPad. Follow the golden dot, fill a shape or paint inside its outline, then save your creation.

Draw, color and count

Explore 72 pictures in 12 themes, numbers 0–9 with countable apples and the English alphabet. Eighteen lessons are free to repeat. One optional purchase unlocks the other 90. No subscription.

A gentle creative space

No ads, accounts or tracking. Spoken guidance in six languages: English, Spanish, German, Russian, French and Brazilian Portuguese. The interface is in English. Drawing works offline; purchases and restoration need an internet connection.

Requires iOS 17 or later. App Store availability will be announced after release.
