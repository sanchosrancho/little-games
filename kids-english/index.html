How we play

One small discovery at a time

01 · Listen

Hear a letter name and a short word. The first letter is highlighted.

02 · Find

One simple challenge: find the right letter among three.

03 · Build

Complete the alphabet to open 18 first words. Tap or drag letters into any matching slot, in any order.

Build at your own pace, in any order. No timer or penalties. Repeating aloud is optional: the app does not record voices or assess pronunciation.
