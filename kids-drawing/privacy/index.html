PRIVACY POLICY · Updated October 6, 2026

Little artists. Big respect for privacy.

Kids Drawing does not collect personal data through the app. No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Drawings and preferences stay in local app storage.

Who we are

Kids Drawing is a drawing and coloring app for young children, provided by Aleksandr Shevliakov (“we”, “us”). For questions about privacy, contact littlegamesios@gmail.com.

Information collected through the app

We do not collect personal data through Kids Drawing. The app has no sign-in or account, and does not request a name, age, email address, contacts, location, microphone or camera access. It does not upload drawings or app settings to our servers.

Drawings and settings on your device

Saved drawings, voice language, sound preferences and other app settings are stored locally on your device. There is no in-app cloud gallery or sharing service. Deleting the app removes its local drawings and settings.

Device backups, if enabled, are managed by Apple under your device and Apple Account settings. A backup may include app data; we do not operate or access those backups.

Optional in-app purchase

Blank paper and 18 lessons are free. One optional, non-consumable purchase unlocks the remaining 90 lessons. There is no subscription.

Apple handles purchases and restoration through the App Store and StoreKit. The app checks Apple’s verified purchase status to provide access to the library. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple processes purchase information under its Privacy Policy. Buying and restoring require an internet connection.

Purchase and restore controls are behind a parental gate. Restoration uses the Apple Account associated with the purchase.

Advertising, analytics and audio

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, third-party SDKs, social features or web views. It does not track users across apps or websites.

Spoken guidance and music are bundled in the app and play locally. Using them does not connect to ElevenLabs, music websites or a speech-generation service. Drawing and coloring work offline.

Children’s privacy

Kids Drawing is designed for young children without collecting personal data through the app. It has no public profiles, chat or online gallery. Settings and purchase controls are protected by a parental gate.

Support is intended for parents and guardians. Please do not send a child’s name, photo, contact details or other personal information when asking for help.

This website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no advertising scripts, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or cookies to these pages. Assets are served from the same website; there are no embedded videos or external font services.

GitHub may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses and device or browser information, to provide and secure its hosting service. Its processing is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link opens a service governed by its own privacy policy.

Support correspondence and your choices

If you email littlegamesios@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that correspondence to answer your request and investigate the issue. We do not use it for advertising or mailing lists.

We retain support correspondence as needed to handle your request and related follow-up, and as required by applicable obligations. You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your support correspondence. Any applicable legal retention requirements may limit deletion.

We cannot retrieve or delete drawings from your device remotely. You control local app data on your device and backups through Apple’s settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the updated version here with a new date. Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact address above.
