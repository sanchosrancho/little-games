Privacy policy

Kids English: ABC & Words · Updated: 14 October 2026

App data

We do not collect or transmit personal data through Kids English. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, microphone access or voice recording.

Progress and narration

Learning progress is stored on the device and is not sent to the developer. Narration is bundled; the app does not contact ElevenLabs during use or assess pronunciation.

Purchases

Apple handles purchases and restoration through the App Store. The developer does not receive payment card details. Paid access is based on verified StoreKit transactions.

Children and parents

The app is designed for children learning with an adult. Purchases and external links are behind a parent challenge. We do not request a child’s name, age, email or voice.

Website and support

This website uses no analytics, cookies or data collection forms. GitHub Pages may process technical information, including IP addresses, under its own privacy policy. If an adult emails support, we receive their email address and message and use them to respond. Do not send a child’s personal information.

Deletion and contact

Deleting the app removes local progress; purchases remain associated with the Apple Account. For privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence: littlegamesios@gmail.com.
