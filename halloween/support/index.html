SUPPORT   ·   Little Halloween for iPhone and iPad

Write to us. A real person answers.

Email littlegamesios@gmail.com and tell us your device, its iOS version and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot. We usually reply within a few days.

How to play

  • Drag a piece onto its matching silhouette. Little ones who find dragging hard can tap a piece first and then tap its place.
  • If a piece lands in the wrong spot, it simply floats back. There is no score, no timer and no way to lose.
  • Identical pieces, like two eyes or the candies, fit any matching spot.
  • In a few decorations a ribbon or frosting goes first, and the pieces that sit on top of it open up next.
  • The grid button in a scene takes you back to the album of all 20 scenes.

Unlocking all scenes

The first 6 scenes are free. A single one-time purchase unlocks all 20 scenes for good. There is no subscription.

To buy, tap a locked scene or the parents button at the top of the album. The parents’ area opens after a simple math problem, so that children cannot start a purchase by accident. The price is shown there in your local currency.

If Ask to Buy is on for your family, the scenes unlock as soon as the request is approved.

Restoring a purchase

Bought the game on another iPhone or iPad, or reinstalled it? Open the parents’ area and tap Restore purchases. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Nothing is charged again.

Refunds

All payments are handled by Apple, so we cannot see or refund them ourselves. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. The game progress stays on the device either way.

Saved progress

Every scene remembers its own pieces, even after the game is closed. Finished scenes get a golden seal in the album and can be played again at any time.

Progress is stored only on the device. It does not move between devices and is removed when the app is deleted.

Sound and motion

Sound and the gentle tilt effect are switched with the buttons at the top of the album; sound can also be turned off during a scene. The tilt effect stays off when Reduce Motion is on in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Motion.

Languages

The game speaks 16 languages and follows the language of the device. To choose another one just for this game, find Little Halloween in iOS Settings and pick a language there.

Devices

Little Halloween runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. It works fully offline; an internet connection is needed only to buy or restore the unlock.
