A few things to know

Can I start from zero?

Yes. Begin with lines and simple forms, then progress toward more detailed scenes. Lessons include where to start and what to check.

Does it work offline?

Lessons, illustrations, the drawing canvas and bundled music work offline. Buying or restoring access requires the App Store and an internet connection.

Are my drawings uploaded?

No. Your drawings, favorites and learning progress stay on your device. The app has no account, advertising, analytics or public gallery.

Can I restore my purchase?

Use Restore purchase in Settings with the same Apple Account. This restores eligible paid access, without charging again. It does not transfer your local drawings.
