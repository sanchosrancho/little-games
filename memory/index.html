What will you play today?

Begin with a few pairs or give your memory a bigger challenge. Every completed round earns a sticker for your album.

Little wins you can keep

From the screen
to your storybook.

Win a round, earn a sticker. Keep your collection in the album, then save an A4 PDF to Files or print stickers and bookmarks together.

Stickers: 45 × 45 mm. Bookmarks: 45 × 140 mm. Print at 100% and cut along the dotted lines. Use full-sheet sticker paper, not pre-cut labels.

More seasons, more stories

Halloween is available now. Memory Club adds new seasonal packs each month through app updates. Future packs are shown as planned until released.
