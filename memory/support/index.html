Kids Memory

A little help, with care.

Answers for players, parents and anyone printing a new collection of friends.

How do I change the game or difficulty?

On the Play screen, choose Pairs, Fireflies or Triples, then select 4, 6, 8, 12 or 18 groups. The Play button stays above the bottom tabs and shows the number of cards.

How does Memory Club work?

Download for free and play the first 6 rounds and 4-group boards in all three games. Memory Club costs €3.99/month in Spain (local prices vary), unlocks all rounds and larger boards, and includes new monthly seasonal packs through app updates. Monthly subscription renews automatically and is charged to your Apple Account. Cancel in App Store settings at least 24 hours before renewal; access remains until the paid period ends. Restore using Settings → Memory Club → Restore purchases after the adult challenge. Earned stickers and printing remain available after cancellation. Do I need an internet connection?

Games, illustrations, music and PDF creation work offline. An internet connection is needed to buy, restore or verify a subscription and download app updates with new packs. Apple manages subscription access.

How do I print stickers or bookmarks?

Finish at least one round and open My album → Prepare to print. Choose stickers or bookmarks, then save or print the PDF. Use A4 paper at 100% scale. Cut along the dotted lines; children should have an adult help with cutting.

How do I change the music or language?

Tap the gear on the Play screen. Music, volume and card sounds have separate controls. Choose one of 20 app languages or use your device language. Music respects Silent Mode and pauses when the app is inactive.

Where is my progress stored?

Your sticker album, completed round numbers and preferences are stored on this device. There is no account or cross-device sync. Removing the app removes its local data; a device backup may restore an older copy. An unfinished round starts again after closing the app.

Where are the other monthly collections?

Halloween is available now. New seasonal packs arrive each month through app updates and are included with an active Memory Club subscription. Future packs remain marked as planned until released.

The app is not behaving as expected. What should I try?

Close and reopen the app, then check for an available update. If the problem continues, email support with your device model, iOS version and the steps that caused it. Please avoid reinstalling before contacting us if you want to keep your local album.

Still need a hand?

Write to littlegamesios@gmail.com. Include the app name, device model, iOS version and a short description. A screenshot can help, but please remove personal information first. Support is intended for adults; do not send a child’s name, photo or contact details.
