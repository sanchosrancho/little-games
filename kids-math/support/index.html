How do we start?

Tap the play button to begin or continue a round. Choose one answer at a time. Tap the speaker to hear the question again; help is available when needed. There is no timer or penalty for trying again.

Use the map to revisit a round. In the parents’ area, choose a starting chapter for your child. This changes the starting point; it does not mark earlier skills as learned or unlock paid content.

Which activities are free?

The first eight rounds and the first round of each of the other four chapters are free: 12 repeatable rounds in total. A monthly or yearly subscription unlocks all 40 initial rounds and new chapters released during the subscription. There is no free trial.

A lock can mean that a subscription is required or that an earlier round in the chapter must be completed. Purchases open only after the parental gate.

Buying, restoring and canceling

Open the parents’ area and select the full adventure. The purchase screen shows the price and billing period before you buy. Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically unless canceled before renewal in your Apple Account settings.

To restore, use Restore purchases with the same Apple Account used to buy and an internet connection. Restoration does not charge you again. If a purchase is awaiting approval, access opens after Apple confirms it. Try again later if the store is unavailable.

Manage or cancel using Manage subscription in the parents’ area, or Apple’s subscription settings. Access continues through the paid period. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Language, voice and music

Open the parents’ area to choose English, Spanish, German, Russian, French or Brazilian Portuguese. Voice, sounds, background music and animation have separate settings. Check device volume if you cannot hear speech. Music becomes quieter during spoken instructions and pauses when the app is not active.

With VoiceOver enabled, the app gives priority to VoiceOver and pauses its background music.

Progress and devices

Fox Steps supports iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. Activities and bundled audio work offline. Buying and restoring require internet.

Progress and stickers are saved on this device. They are not synchronized across devices. Restoring a subscription restores access, not another device’s progress. Reset progress in the parents’ area removes local learning progress; deleting the app removes its local data. Apple-managed device backups are separate.

Subscription expiration does not remove saved stickers or progress. Free rounds remain available.

About the background music

Forest Lofi is an original instrumental loop made for Fox Steps, with soft keys, bass and gentle percussion. It is bundled locally; no music-streaming account is needed.
