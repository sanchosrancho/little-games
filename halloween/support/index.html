Choosing a world

Little Halloween becomes Little Puzzle Worlds in version 2.0. On the home screen, choose Halloween or Underwater World. Use All worlds in an album to switch. Each world has 20 scenes and remembers its own progress.

The two-world update is coming soon to the App Store. If you still have version 1.0.1, it contains the original Halloween world.

How to play

Drag a large piece to its matching silhouette, or tap the piece and then its place. A miss sends the piece back. There is no score, timer or losing. The grid button returns to the world’s album.

Unlocking a world

The first 6 scenes of each world are free. A separate one-time purchase per world unlocks that world’s other 14 scenes. No subscription is required.

Choose a world and tap scene 7 or the parents button in its album. Answer the simple addition question to see that world’s local price, purchase button and Restore purchases. An internet connection is needed to load product information, buy or restore.

If Ask to Buy is enabled, access opens after Apple confirms the approved purchase.

Already bought Halloween?

Your previous Halloween purchase continues to unlock all 20 Halloween scenes. The update keeps the same app and Halloween product identifier. Your Halloween progress is also preserved when updating the app.

Underwater World is a separate purchase and is not included in the original Halloween unlock.

Restoring purchases

Open a world’s parents’ area, answer the addition question and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. A Halloween purchase restores Halloween; an Ocean purchase restores Underwater World.

If restoration fails, check your connection and that in-app purchases are allowed on the device. Apple’s purchase-restoration help has additional steps.

Saved progress and devices

Every scene remembers its own placed pieces. Returning to another world, rotating or folding a supported device keeps your progress. Finished scenes can be played again.

Progress is saved locally. The app has no cloud sync between devices. Deleting the app removes local progress, although Apple-managed device backups may contain app data.

Little Puzzle Worlds runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. Version 2.0 adapts to wide and narrow layouts, including iPhone Duo folding states.

Music, sound and languages

The note button controls background music independently of sound effects. Preferences are remembered, and music pauses when the app goes into the background. The tilt effect can be disabled and respects Reduce Motion.

The app supports 16 languages and follows your app/device language setting. In iOS Settings, find Little Puzzle Worlds to choose another available language for the app.

Refunds and help

Apple processes payments and refund requests. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

For help with the game, email littlegamesios@gmail.com with the app version, device model and a description of the issue. Read our privacy policy for information about support messages.
