Privacy policy

How information is handled in Lumi.

Updated: 23 October 2026

About this policy

Lumi: Lagoon Jump is developed by Aleksandr Shevliakov. This policy explains how the iPhone game and this website handle information. Contact: littlegamesios@gmail.com.

Playing the game

Lumi has no in-game registration, advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. The game does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone, or location. We do not automatically receive your gameplay data.

Progress on your device

Scores, settings, collectibles, currencies, upgrades, and purchase transaction identifiers are stored locally on your device using app storage and the iOS Keychain. We do not operate a cloud account or a server wallet. Ordinary progress may be removed when you delete the app; purchase records and balance data in the Keychain may remain after reinstallation. Consumable balances do not sync between devices.

Tilt controls

If you choose tilt controls, the game uses device-motion readings during play to guide Lumi. The readings are processed on your iPhone and are not recorded or sent to us. Touch controls are also available.

Purchases and Apple services

Apple processes optional purchases and restoration through StoreKit. The app receives verified transaction information to grant items and process revocations. We do not receive payment card details. Apple and TestFlight may process information under their own policies, including diagnostics and beta feedback you share through Apple.

Website hosting

This website has no analytics, advertising cookies, contact forms, or embedded third-party content. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs under its own privacy statement. Following an external link opens a service with its own policy.

Support and retention

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and investigate your request, retain them only as needed for support and any applicable obligations, and do not use them for advertising. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information.

Children and privacy

We do not request a child’s name, age, contact information, or other personal details in the game. A parent or guardian can contact us about support or privacy without sharing a child’s personal information.

Your choices and contact

You can use touch instead of tilt and manage purchase permissions in iOS. To ask about personal information in support correspondence or request its deletion, email us. We cannot remotely access or erase locally stored game data; contact us for help with local storage. Apple manages Apple Account and purchase records.

Changes

We will update this page when the way the app or website handles information changes.
