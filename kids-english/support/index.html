Support

Contact

Email littlegamesios@gmail.com with the device model, iOS and app versions and a short description. Do not send passwords, payment details or a child’s personal information.

Getting started

Choose a letter and tap its card to hear the name and word. Repeating is optional; Дальше к игре opens the activity. Word games open after completing all 26 letters with full course access.

Full course

A–E are free. One purchase unlocks F–Z and word activities after completing the alphabet. Open Родителям, solve the challenge and choose the purchase. There is no subscription.

Restore purchase

In Родителям, tap Восстановить покупку using the same Apple Account and an internet connection. Pending purchases unlock after Apple approval. Refunds revoke paid access.

Sound

Check device volume and tap the card again. English recordings are bundled in the app; Russian instructions are displayed as text. Audio stops when the app goes into the background.

Progress and refunds

Progress stays on this device and does not sync between devices. Deleting the app may remove progress. Apple handles refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com.
