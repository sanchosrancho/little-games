PRIVACY · Little Puzzle Worlds · Updated November 4, 2026

Little worlds. Big respect for privacy.

No personal-data collection from gameplay. No accounts, advertising or tracking. Progress and preferences are stored locally; Apple handles purchases.

Who we are

Little Puzzle Worlds, previously named Little Halloween, is a collection of gentle puzzle worlds for children aged 2–5, developed by Aleksandr Shevliakov, an individual developer (“we”, “us”). This policy also covers earlier Little Halloween versions of the same app.

Contact: littlegamesios@gmail.com.

Information collected by the app

We do not collect personal information from gameplay. The app has no sign-in, child profiles, advertising, analytics or tracking tools. It does not request names, ages, email addresses, photos, contacts, microphone, camera or location access.

We do not operate an app server or receive your child’s play activity. Apple’s services handle purchases and restoration as described below.

Progress, preferences and motion

  • Local progress. The selected world, completed scenes and placed pieces are saved in the app’s local storage. Each world has its own progress.
  • Preferences. Music, sound effects and optional motion settings are remembered on the device. Music is a bundled audio file; it is not streamed.
  • Motion. When enabled, device motion produces a gentle tilt effect. Readings are processed on the device and are not recorded or transmitted. The app respects Reduce Motion.

The app provides no cloud sync service. Apple may include local app data in device backups, depending on your settings. Removing the app removes its local data; restoring an Apple-managed backup may restore it.

Separate one-time purchases

In version 2.0, Halloween and Underwater World each contain 20 scenes. The first 6 scenes of each world are free; a separate non-consumable purchase for that world unlocks its remaining 14 scenes. There are no subscriptions.

An existing Halloween purchase continues to unlock Halloween. It does not include Underwater World or other separately sold worlds.

Purchases and restoration use Apple’s App Store and StoreKit services. The app receives verified purchase status to decide which scenes are unlocked. We do not receive payment-card details or Apple Account login credentials. Apple handles payment information under its Privacy Policy.

Purchase and restore controls are in the parents’ area, behind a simple addition question. The local StoreKit price is shown before purchase.

Advertising, analytics and Apple services

The app contains no ads, third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, social features or embedded web views. It does not track people across apps or websites.

Apple may make crash diagnostics and aggregated App Store statistics available to developers under Apple’s own policies and your device settings. The app does not add its own analytics or crash-reporting service.

Children’s privacy

The app is designed for young children without creating an account or supplying personal information. We do not build profiles of children or collect their gameplay data.

If you contact support

If a parent or guardian emails us, we receive the sender’s email address and the information included in the message. We use that information to respond to the support request. This is separate from gameplay; the app does not collect an email address.

Your choices

You can switch music and effects off independently, disable the optional tilt effect, and manage app backups through your device settings. You can remove local progress by deleting the app; it has no account to delete.

For questions about a support message you sent us, contact littlegamesios@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

Updates to this policy will be published here with a new effective date. The change from Little Halloween to Little Puzzle Worlds adds worlds and preferences; it does not add personal-data collection from gameplay.
