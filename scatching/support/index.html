Scatching for everyone support

Get in touch

Email littlegamesios@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, app version and a short description. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Get started

Start with a short warmup in Studio, then choose a subject in Lessons or Library. Prepare your sketchbook and materials, read Where to start and follow the illustrated steps at your own pace. The built-in canvas offers optional practice on screen, with Apple Pencil support on iPad.

Full Studio

Four starter lessons and every drawing example are free. One non-consumable purchase unlocks all 83 lessons. There is no subscription. The final price appears in the App Store before purchase.

Restore access

Open Settings → Restore purchase, use the same Apple Account and connect to the internet. Pending purchases unlock after Apple approval. Refunds revoke paid access; they do not remove your drawings.

Drawings and music

Drawings and progress stay on this device; they do not sync between devices. Music is off by default. Enable it with the music button or in Settings, check volume and silent mode. Music pauses when the app is in the background.

Refunds

Apple handles refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue App Store refunds directly.
