Privacy policy · Updated October 11, 2026

Who we are

Scatching for everyone is provided by Aleksandr Shevliakov. For privacy questions, email littlegamesios@gmail.com.

Data in the app

We do not collect personal data through the app. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, public profiles or uploads. Lessons, artwork rendering and bundled music run on your device. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photo library.

Your drawings and settings

Drawings, lesson progress, favorites, language and audio preferences are stored locally on your device. There is no app-operated cloud sync. Deleting the app removes its local data. Apple-managed device backups may include app data according to your settings; we do not operate or access those backups.

One-time App Store purchase

Apple processes payments and restores through StoreKit. The app verifies signed Apple transactions on your device to determine access. We do not receive your card or billing details, and purchase records are not sent to our servers. Apple handles its services under its own Privacy Policy.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your email address and information you choose to send. We use it to answer and investigate your request, not for marketing. We retain correspondence as needed to resolve and follow up on the request and meet applicable obligations. You may request access, correction or deletion at the same email address, subject to legal retention requirements. We cannot access your local drawings remotely. Please avoid sending sensitive information or children’s personal data.

This website

This static site adds no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, external fonts or application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in to GitHub. Its processing is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Apple may provide diagnostics according to your device sharing settings; the app has no separate diagnostics service.

Your choices and changes

You can delete local data by removing the app, adjust device backup and diagnostic settings, and contact us about support correspondence. If our privacy practices change, we will update this page and its date.
