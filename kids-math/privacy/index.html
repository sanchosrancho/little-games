Respect for privacy.

Fox Steps does not collect personal data through the app. Learning progress stays on your device.

Who we are

Fox Steps: First Math (also identified as Math Kids in development builds) is provided by Aleksandr Shevliakov (“we”, “us”). For privacy questions, email littlegamesios@gmail.com.

Information in the app

The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking tools, chat or public profiles. It does not ask for your child’s name, age, email address, location, contacts, camera or microphone. It does not upload learning progress to our servers.

Completed rounds, collected stickers, recent learning difficulties and voice, music and language preferences are stored locally. You can reset learning progress in the parents’ area. Deleting the app removes its local data. Apple-managed device backups may include app data, depending on your settings; we do not operate or access those backups.

Purchases

Optional monthly or yearly subscriptions are processed by Apple using StoreKit. The app checks Apple’s verified purchase status to unlock subscribed content. We do not receive payment card details. Buying or restoring requires internet and uses your Apple Account. Apple processes purchase information under its Privacy Policy.

Subscription expiration or cancellation does not erase learning progress or stickers. Purchase, restore and subscription management controls are behind a parental gate.

Audio and offline use

Spoken guidance and original background music are bundled with the app and played on the device. Playback does not send text or a child’s voice to ElevenLabs or any voice-generation service. The app does not record audio. Learning activities work offline.

Children’s privacy

The app is designed for young children without collecting their personal data. Support is intended for parents and guardians. Please do not send a child’s name, photo, recording or other personal information when contacting us. If you believe a child’s personal information was sent to us, contact us so we can address it.

This website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no cookies, advertising, analytics scripts or tracking pixels. Images and styles are served from this site, without embedded videos or external font services.

GitHub may process request information, including IP addresses and browser or device information, to provide its hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links open services with their own policies.

Support correspondence and your choices

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond and investigate your request, on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing support. Our email provider processes correspondence to deliver the service. We do not use support messages for advertising or mailing lists.

We retain correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and related follow-up, and where required by applicable obligations. Email us to request access, correction or deletion. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, data portability, and to complain to a data protection authority. Legal retention requirements may limit deletion.

We cannot access, recover or delete your local learning progress remotely. You control local data and Apple-managed backups on your device.

Changes

We will publish changes to this policy on this page with an updated date. Contact littlegamesios@gmail.com with questions.
