Your garden on this device

Progress is stored locally. There is no developer-operated cross-device garden sync. Include your device model and app version when contacting support.

Restore your purchase

Open garden selection → full version → Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account. This restores paid access, not a garden from another device.

Sound

Tap Play forest and check both the device and in-game volume. Sounds pause when the app leaves the foreground.

Harvest and growth

Tap ripe produce or Harvest. After picking, use fertilizer or the next morning. An empty plant cannot be harvested again.
