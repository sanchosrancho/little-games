Start a local match

Put the device between the players, choose local play and a table, and let each player confirm they are ready. Drag the paddle on your half of the screen. A match ends at seven points.

Play on your own

Choose the computer as your opponent and pick one of three difficulty levels. Local and computer games do not require internet or a Game Center account.

Invite a friend on another device

Both players need the beta installed, internet and Game Center signed in. Use the friend invitation option in the game and accept the Game Center invitation on the other device. Online play is in beta; testing across two physical devices is still in progress. If a match disconnects, return to the menu and send a new invitation.

If something goes wrong

Reopen the game and check for an available TestFlight update. For online issues, check internet and Game Center sign-in on both devices. Tell us your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, game build number and the steps that caused the problem. A screenshot is helpful if it contains no personal information.

Beta access and purchases

The game is currently in a closed TestFlight beta. Classic, all computer levels and the regular cup are free. The optional Full version unlocks five extra tables and Arcade Cup through one non-consumable purchase. The release price is €2.99 in Spain; the App Store shows your local price. Choose Full version in the catalogue and Restore purchase to recover access with the same Apple Account. TestFlight purchases are sandbox tests and do not charge real money. To request beta access, email us using the button above; access is not automatic.
