Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

About this policy

Table Rivals: Pocket Pong is developed by Aleksandr Shevliakov. This policy covers the iPhone/iPad app and this website, including the current closed beta. For questions or data requests, email littlegamesios@gmail.com.

Your game stays on your device

The app stores preferences such as sound and computer difficulty, and your best rally, locally on your device. We do not run a server to receive or sync this information. Device backups may include it according to your Apple settings. Local two-player games and computer matches can be played offline.

Optional online play with Game Center

Playing a friend on a separate device uses Apple’s Game Center and requires internet. The game uses a player display name and Game Center player identifier to show and connect opponents, and exchanges match state such as paddle and ball positions, readiness and scores through GameKit. We do not send that data to a developer-operated server. Apple processes Game Center account, activity and service data under its own rules; visibility and multiplayer restrictions can be managed in Apple settings. See Apple’s Game Center privacy notice.

No advertising or analytics SDKs

The app contains no advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, chat or user-uploaded content. You do not create a separate Table Rivals account. The optional Full version is a one-time purchase processed by Apple through StoreKit. The app checks verified purchase entitlements and can restore access with your Apple Account. We do not receive payment card details or send purchase history to a developer-operated server. Apple may process TestFlight diagnostics and feedback under its privacy policy.

This website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics, cookies, tracking pixels, external fonts or embedded videos. GitHub may process request information such as IP addresses for hosting and security under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. All visual assets and scripts are served with the site.

When you email us

Email links open your own mail app. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your question or arrange beta access, retain it only as needed for that purpose, and do not sell it or add you to a marketing list. You can ask us to correct or delete our copy by email.

Your choices

Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to any device backups you keep. We cannot retrieve local game records remotely. Manage Game Center account information, visibility and child multiplayer restrictions through Apple’s settings. Please do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information in a support email.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if data handling changes. Contact: Aleksandr Shevliakov, littlegamesios@gmail.com.
