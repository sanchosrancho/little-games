Kids Memory privacy policy · Updated October 9, 2026

Who we are

Kids Memory is a memory card game for ages 4 and up, provided by Aleksandr Shevliakov (“we”, “us”). For privacy questions, contact littlegamesios@gmail.com.

Information collected through the app

We do not collect personal data through Kids Memory. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not ask for your name, age, email, contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone access. Gameplay, music and illustration rendering happen on your device; they do not connect to our servers.

Album and settings on your device

The app stores earned stickers, completed round numbers, language and audio preferences locally. There is no app-operated cloud sync. Removing the app removes its local data. Apple device backups, if enabled, may include app data; you manage them in your device and Apple Account settings. We do not operate or access those backups.

Printing and sharing

When you choose to export your album, the app creates a PDF locally containing the selected animal illustrations and print labels. It does not add your name, account details or device identifiers. The PDF is passed to the iOS share or print menu only when you choose to share, save or print it. Any selected destination, printer or other app handles that file under its own policies. Copies saved outside the app remain there until you remove them.

Children’s privacy

The game is designed to be usable without collecting personal information from children or adults. It has no public profiles, chat, user uploads, in-app purchases or advertising. Support correspondence is intended for adults. Please do not include a child’s personal information in a support request.

This website and Apple services

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no cookies, advertising, tracking pixels or analytics scripts. Images and fonts do not load from external marketing services. GitHub may process ordinary request information to deliver and secure the website; see its Privacy Statement. Apple may provide diagnostic or aggregated app information according to your device sharing settings and its Privacy Policy. The app does not implement a separate diagnostics service.

Support correspondence and your choices

If you email littlegamesios@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We use it to respond and investigate your request, not for advertising or mailing lists. Correspondence is retained as needed for support and related follow-up, and to meet applicable obligations. You can request access, correction or deletion by writing to the same address; legal retention requirements may limit deletion. We cannot retrieve or delete your local sticker album remotely.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s privacy practices change, we will update this page and its date. Please contact us with questions about this policy.
