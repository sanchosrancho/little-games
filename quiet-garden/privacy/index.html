Privacy policy

The Garden · Updated: 19 October 2026

Who is responsible

The Garden is developed by Aleksandr Shevliakov. For privacy questions, contact littlegamesios@gmail.com.

Data in the app

The Garden does not send gameplay or personal information to the developer. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, chat or user uploads. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Your garden and settings

Garden progress, harvest, prepared dishes, language and sound preferences are stored on your device. The app uses a local WebKit game view and local app storage; the game does not load a remote website or stream audio. Nature sounds are generated on the device. We do not provide our own cloud synchronization. Your device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Purchases

The full garden is a one-time, non-consumable App Store purchase. Apple processes payments and restoration. We do not receive your payment card details. The app uses verified StoreKit transactions to determine paid access. Purchases and restoration may require an internet connection. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

This website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics, tracking pixels, cookies, embedded videos or external font services. GitHub may process request information, including IP addresses and browser/device information, to operate and secure its hosting. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Support correspondence

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and investigate the issue, not for advertising or mailing lists. Please do not send sensitive information. We retain correspondence only as needed for support, related follow-up and applicable obligations. You may request access, correction or deletion by email.

Your choices and deletion

You can turn nature sounds off in the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data, subject to any device backups you maintain. We cannot retrieve or delete your garden remotely. An App Store purchase remains associated with your Apple Account and can be restored. Manage backups and App Store purchases through Apple’s settings.

Children

The Garden is a general-audience gardening game. We do not ask for a child’s name, age or contact information and do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app.

Changes

We will publish updates to this policy on this page with a revised date.
